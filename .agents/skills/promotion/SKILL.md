# Promotion and distribution (optional)

## When to use
Product launch, awareness, public content, social channels, outreach, campaign tracking or marketing experiments.

## Procedure
1. Read vision, target audience, positioning, claims and actual product readiness. Identify appropriate communities/channels and their rules; do not assume access to private networks or contacts.
2. Define measurable campaign goal, audience, value proposition, channel strategy, timeline and modest KPI set.
3. Maintain campaign artifacts in one versioned location, e.g. `docs/promotion/`: brief, message drafts, public opportunity list, publication log, observed metrics. Distinguish proposed/drafted/approved/published.
4. Prepare truthful, contextual messages and calls to action; verify claims. Avoid spam, bulk unsolicited contact, invented engagement and sensitive contact lists.
5. Require explicit authorization before posting, scheduling publication, sending email/DM or changing an external account. Verify actual action URL/status afterward.
6. Coordinate `automation` for recurring discovery of relevant conversations, response tracking and KPI summaries; discovery is not authorization to intervene.
7. Evaluate observed reach, conversion and qualitative feedback; record unknown as unknown, not zero. Feed product insights back into issues.

## Boundaries
No mandatory campaign for technical projects. No dependency on Metricool, Gmail or any social provider; use only available, authorized connectors.
